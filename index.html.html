About Dr. Manju K. G.

A Journey of Academic Excellence

With a rich academic journey spanning over two and a half decades, Dr. Manju K. G. has made significant contributions to higher education through teaching, research, curriculum development, and institutional leadership. Her expertise bridges the domains of Business Administration, Computer Science, and Management.

Having served in prestigious leadership roles including Vice Principal and Associate Professor across institutions affiliated to Anna University, Kerala University, and CUSAT, she brings a wealth of experience in academic administration, examination duties, and institutional coordination.
